How it works

Scan and survey together

Two kinds of data, each used for what it does best.

  1. Scan

    A Gaussian splat is millions of small coloured blobs that together look like the real place: trees, walls, the road, the ruins. It is only a skin, though. Cut it and it is hollow, with soft, ragged edges, and under trees there may be no ground in it at all.

  2. Survey

    The drone survey on this site is bare earth: the trees are removed and the ground is measured. That is where the solid ground in the cut and the section drawing come from, so the levels are the same as on every other page.

  3. Alignment

    The scan is fitted to the survey once, by matching the ground the scan can see to the surveyed ground, and the fit is saved with the site. For your own scan, set the turn roughly by eye, then press Find it on the survey.

  4. Draw

    Use Show behind the cut to see just a slice of the site, and Hide above to take off the tree canopy. Download the view and the section drawing at the same scale to trace over.
