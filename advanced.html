Sections cut through a 3D scan of the site, projecting drawings onto a physical model, and adding a new site. Students don't need these for a first site analysis: contour drawings, sun studies and models all come from Contours, Sun & climate, the Model kit and Your model. Come here when your tutor sends you, or when you want to go further.
