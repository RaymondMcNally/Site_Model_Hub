01 · Terrain

Read the ground

Either an STL of the ground surface with north up (+Y), or one or more GeoTIFF elevation tiles, such as the free GSI open lidar for Ireland (use the DTM, bare earth). For an STL, say what scale it was exported at. For GeoTIFFs, drag a box round the area you want: heights, location and north come from the tiles.

03 · Features

Trace what's on the site

Watercourses, roads, paths, buildings and the site boundary appear on every drawing. Add a note to any feature and it becomes a "find it on the plan" exercise on the contours page. Mark one as the main feature: pages centre on it.

Drawing · click to add points · double-click to finish

    Drag a point to move it. Shift-click a point to remove it.

    Import from QGIS or CAD

    For a site read from GeoTIFF tiles, map metres and longitude/latitude are placed exactly from the tiles' coordinates (the corner fields fill themselves in). For an STL, longitude/latitude is placed using the latitude and longitude above as the centre of the survey, so it's approximate; use the shift to line it up with the contours.
