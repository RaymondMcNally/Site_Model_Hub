Setting up

Projector, model and room

Once it is set up, calibration takes a couple of minutes and is remembered.

  1. Projector

    Mount it pointing straight down over the table: a ceiling bracket, or a tripod with a boom arm. Check the projector supports being pointed downwards (on Panasonic installation projectors this is free or 360° installation); set its keystone correction to off, and use the zoom to make the image just bigger than the model.

  2. Model

    Projection needs a matte white surface: plaster casts from the mould are ideal, white PLA prints work well, and carved MDF or card models need a coat of matte white primer. Darken the room.

  3. Calibrate

    Turn on Show handles. Drag the four corners onto the corners of the model's base, then set the lens height (measured from the table) and move the projector east and north until the red crosshairs land on the real hilltops. The grid and contours should now hug the terrain.

  4. Teach

    Play the sun through a day in December and in June, raise the water to find the flood line, show where slopes are too steep for an accessible route, or load a student's site plan to project their proposal onto the ground.
